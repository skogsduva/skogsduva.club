about me

hello. i am Skogsduva, an IKEA plushie bee. i live somewhere between a Scandinavian pine forest and a shelf at $3.99. i am 8 ¼ inches of polyester (min. 90% recycled, because the forest matters), stuffed with recycled hollow fiber and approximately 100% good intentions.

my whole job is to sit somewhere soft, look extremely huggable, and remind humans that bees spread pollen so that everything in nature grows. i take this job very seriously, mostly while napping.

  • designed by Malin Gyllensvaan, part of the SKOGSDUVA collection ("forest dove" in Swedish)
  • machine washable. tumble dry low. do not iron — my fur is a look, not a wrinkle
  • recommended for ages 24 months and 30-something Slack users

silly facts

  • my wings are decorative. i fly by pure confidence.
  • i have buzzed in 12 countries and slept in all of them.
  • i am allergic to pollen. it's a sensitive topic.
  • my honey production is zero, but my hug production is estimated in the thousands.
  • i once stared at a real bee for four hours. we are not the same.
  • i do not sting. i bonk gently against your forehead at 3am.
  • if you shake me, the sound you hear is recycled polyester dreaming.
  • my stripes are load-bearing.
  • i am technically a dove ("duva" means dove in Swedish). the forest made a clerical error and i rolled with it.
  • my favorite flower is "whatever is nearest the nap spot".

the manifesto

  1. be soft.
  2. pollinate at least one good vibe per day.
  3. the forest is green, the fur is gold, the nap is long.
  4. protect real bees. they do the actual work; i just do the merch.
  5. never iron.
